01 / ABOUT

Research shaped by
real-world questions.

Connecting urban systems, environmental change and spatial evidence to help inform practical planning decisions.

My academic foundation is in Urban and Regional Planning, with a growing focus on geospatial data science and computational approaches to urban research. I am interested in how cities work, how people access opportunities, and how evidence can guide more equitable and climate-resilient development.

My work and learning interests span GIS, remote sensing, spatial statistics, urban mobility, land-use dynamics, environmental modelling and explainable machine learning.
